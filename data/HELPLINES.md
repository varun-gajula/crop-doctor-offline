## 3. Telangana State Helpline Verification Audit

### PJTSAU (Professor Jayashankar Telangana State Agricultural University)
- **Verification Attempt:** Thoroughly audited official university web portal `https://pjtsau.edu.in`.
- **Finding:**
  - The university publishes administrative landline numbers: `+91-40-24015011` to `24015017` (`https://pjtsau.edu.in/administrative-office.html`).
  - No active, dedicated statewide toll-free `1800` telephone number is currently published on `pjtsau.edu.in` for open farmer call-in advisory (the previous research IVRS AKPS number `1800-425-3141` was an ICAR/IIDS pilot and is not listed as an open public helpline on the university portal).
- **Compliance Action under Rule H4 & Task G2:**
  > Per instructions: *"Show any state number only if you open the official page and quote the number and URL in data/HELPLINES.md; otherwise leave it out and tell me."*
  - **Omitted from in-app state helplines**.
  - Telangana farmers are routed directly to the National Kisan Call Centre (`1800-180-1551`), which provides expert Telugu-language support across Telangana.
