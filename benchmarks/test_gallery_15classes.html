🌿 Crop Doctor: 15 Classes Phone Testing Gallery

Point your Android phone (POCO M4 5G) at each photo card on this screen to test real-time vision inference & advisory.

How to test with your phone:
1. Open Crop Doctor on your phone (Airplane Mode ON ✈️).
2. Tap "Scan Leaf" on the Home screen.
3. Point your phone camera directly at the image card on your laptop monitor within the green frame.
4. Press the large green shutter button.
5. Watch the app detect the crop, condition, severity, and multi-lingual advisory in under 1.5 seconds!
